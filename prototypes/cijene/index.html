Pricing on the site: five ways to show it

Each page is a full copy of the current homepage with one way of showing prices added. All five use the same made-up price sheet, so you are comparing the presentation, not the numbers. Use ‹ › in the top bar to move between them. Look at them on a phone too.

  1. 00Current versionfor comparison

    Today's homepage, unchanged.

  2. 01Prvi korakwoven in · quietest

    No new section. Prices sit where the decision is made: a "from" line on each service card, a price on each process step (free call → 300 € sketch, credited against the build → fixed quote → hosting per month), and a price next to each consulting answer. The build price itself only appears as a floor and a typical range.

  3. 02Cjenikrate card + FAQ

    One compact "Koliko to košta?" section: a starting price for each service, the typical project range, what makes a build cost more, and four price questions answered in plain sentences, the way people (and AI tools) ask them.

  4. 03Primjeripriced examples

    No price list. Six typical jobs (bakery, car service, accounting…) with what each does, how long it takes and roughly what it costs, placed on one price scale. Visitors find the job closest to theirs. Needs real projects to be honest in the long run.

  5. 04Razinethree levels

    Three levels for each service, one service at a time: build size, hosting and support level (with response times), consulting session, package or security review. The middle level is marked as the usual one. This is how a bigger client ends up paying more: more scope and faster support.

  6. 05Procjenaestimator

    Three questions give an instant range (never a single number), the build time and when it pays for itself. The visitor can send the estimate by e-mail in one tap. The rules behind the range are written out underneath.
